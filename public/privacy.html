Privacy Policy

Empower Nyamagabe respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Donors: name, phone number and/or email, and donation records (amount, family code, need, payment reference and status).
  • Local leaders: name, contact details and sector, plus a record of the families they submit and verification decisions they make.
  • Families: the household head's name, optional phone number, cell and village, Ubudehe category, household size, the need, and the leader's supporting notes. These are submitted by local leaders, with the family's consent.
  • Basic technical logs (e.g., browser/device info) to keep the service secure.

What donors can and cannot see

To protect families' dignity, donors and the public see only an anonymous family code, the sector, the Ubudehe category, the need type and amount, and a short public description. A family's name, phone number, cell, village and the leader's private notes are visible only to authorised local leaders (in their own sector) and the administrator.

How we use information

  • To create and manage accounts and to verify that a family has a genuine need.
  • To record each donation and link it to the family and need it paid for, so donors can trace it.
  • To produce anonymous summary reports and, for the capstone pilot, research findings in which participants are identified only by codes.
  • To improve security and fix errors.

Payments

Payments are handled by Flutterwave on its hosted checkout page, using Mobile Money, card or other methods it supports. We never collect or store Mobile Money numbers, PINs or card numbers. We store only the transaction reference, amount, payment status, and the family and purpose the donation is linked to. During the pilot, payments run in Flutterwave's test mode and no real money moves.

Pilot participation and your rights

Taking part in the pilot is voluntary and you may withdraw at any time without penalty. Taking part or not taking part never affects whether a family is selected to receive help. Data is used only for this research project, is stored securely, and is deleted after the project and its reporting are complete, in line with Rwanda's data protection principles.
