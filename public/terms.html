Terms of Service

By using Empower Nyamagabe, you agree to the terms below. These terms are written in plain language and are meant to protect both users and the project.

Use of the platform

  • You agree to provide accurate information when creating an account.
  • You will not misuse the platform or try to access accounts or data that are not yours.
  • Local leader accounts are created by the administrator. Leaders agree to submit and verify families honestly and to keep private family details confidential.
  • You will not try to identify the families shown on the platform.

Donations

Each donation is made to one verified family and one need (school fees or Mutuelle de Santé), and cannot exceed what that need still requires. Donations are processed by Flutterwave and processing fees may apply depending on the payment method. We record each donation so that you can trace it from your account.

Verification

Families appear to donors only after a local leader or the administrator has approved them. Verification relies on community knowledge and the Ubudehe classification, which can be imperfect. Decisions are logged and can be reviewed.

Pilot status

Empower Nyamagabe is a capstone research pilot. While in pilot, payments run in Flutterwave test mode and no real money is transferred. Moving to real donations would require a live Flutterwave account, appropriate approvals and the families' and local leaders' agreement. We may update these terms as the platform develops.
