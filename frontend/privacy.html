Privacy policy

What Empower Nyamagabe collects, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Donors: name, phone number and/or email, and your donation records (amount, family code, need, payment reference and status).
  • Local leaders: name, contact details and sector, and a record of the families they submit and the decisions they make.
  • Families: the head of household's name, an optional phone number, cell and village, Ubudehe category, household size, the need and the leader's notes. Leaders submit this with the family's consent.
  • Basic technical logs, such as browser and device details, to keep the service secure.

What donors can see

Donors and the public see a family code, the sector, the Ubudehe category, the need and amount, and a short description. A family's name, phone number, cell, village and the leader's notes are only visible to leaders in that sector and to the administrator.

How we use it

  • To run accounts and confirm that a family's need is real.
  • To record each donation against the family and need it paid for.
  • To produce summary reports in which people are identified only by codes.
  • To keep the platform secure and fix problems.

Payments

Payments are handled by Flutterwave on its own checkout page. We never collect or store Mobile Money numbers, PINs or card numbers. We keep the transaction reference, amount, status, and the family and need the donation is for.

Your rights

Using the platform is voluntary. You can ask us to correct your details or close your account at any time. Whether or not someone uses the platform never affects whether a family is chosen for help. Data is stored securely and handled in line with Rwanda's law on the protection of personal data and privacy.
