Giving you can follow from start to finish

Empower Nyamagabe helps families in Nyamagabe District with two costs that decide a lot: school fees, and Mutuelle de Santé, the community health insurance that covers a household when someone falls ill. When a family can't afford both, children are often the ones who leave school.

Families are put forward by Umudugudu and Ubudehe leaders who know them. Every donation is recorded against one family and one need.

  • Every family is approved by a local leader before you can see it.
  • Names, phone numbers and villages are never shown to donors.
  • No bank account needed. Mobile Money works.

What your donation pays for

School feesTerm fees for children in primary and secondary school.
Mutuelle de SantéThe yearly health insurance contribution for a household.
Nothing elseYour money goes to the need you chose, and your history shows which family received it.

How a family gets support

  1. A leader puts a family forward

    An Umudugudu or Ubudehe committee member records the household's Ubudehe category, what it needs and why.

  2. A second person checks it

    Another leader or the administrator approves or rejects it. Only approved families are shown.

  3. You donate

    Pay with Mobile Money or card on Flutterwave's checkout page. We never see your payment details.

  4. You see where it went

    Your history shows the family code, the need and the payment reference for every donation.

Why Nyamagabe

Just over half of Nyamagabe's people (51.4%) live below the poverty line, almost twice the national rate of 27.4%. For many households that means children leaving school and families falling behind on health insurance.

When a family can't pay for both, one of them goes unpaid. Studies in Rwanda link health insurance with lower school dropout.

  • Children leave schoolDropout is 7% in primary, 11% in lower secondary and 6% in upper secondary. Primary repetition is 32%, against 21% nationally.
  • Some families are still uninsuredCoverage rose from 75.2% in 2017 to 96.1% in 2024. The remaining 3.9% is about 14,500 people.
  • Cash gifts leave no traceInternational platforms usually need a bank card, and cash leaves no record of where the money went.

Sources: NISR EICV7 Poverty Profile (2024); Nyamagabe District Development Strategy 2024–2029.

How we keep it honest

Checked by the community

Families are assessed with Rwanda's Ubudehe categories. A rejection needs a written reason, and every decision is logged.

Every donation recorded

Each donation is tied to one family and one need, with its Flutterwave reference and a receipt you can download.

Payment details stay with Flutterwave

Mobile Money numbers, PINs and card numbers are entered only on Flutterwave's page, never on ours.

Families keep their privacy

Donors never see a family's name, phone number or village. Only the need and the sector.

Pick a family and cover one need.

Even a 100 RWF donation can change a family’s life. Every contribution, big or small, can help a child stay in school and a family access health insurance.

Reviewing a family

Decisions are based on the Ubudehe classification and what you know about households in your village. The platform records who submitted each family and who decided, so decisions can be checked later.

  • Check the household is in a lower Ubudehe category and has a real school fee or Mutuelle de Santé need.
  • Keep the public description anonymous. Donors read it, so leave out names, phone numbers and the village.
  • Give a reason when you reject. It is saved in the audit log.
  • Taking part is voluntary. A family's choice never affects whether it gets other help.
