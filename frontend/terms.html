Terms of service

By using Empower Nyamagabe you agree to these terms. They protect donors, local leaders and the families we support.

Using the platform

  • Give accurate information when you create an account.
  • Don't misuse the platform or try to reach accounts or data that aren't yours.
  • Local leader accounts are created by the administrator. Leaders agree to submit and review families honestly and keep family details confidential.
  • Don't try to identify the families shown on the platform.

Donations

Each donation goes to one verified family and one need, and can't be more than that need still requires. Donations are processed by Flutterwave, and fees may apply depending on how you pay. Every donation is recorded so you can see it in your account.

Verification

Families are shown only after a local leader or the administrator has approved them. Verification relies on community knowledge and the Ubudehe classification, which aren't always perfect. Every decision is logged and can be reviewed.

Changes

We may update these terms as the platform grows. The latest version is always on this page.
